As found

Marble, tile, steel and timber

From the walk-through: a hexagon marble floor from the glass, a strip of wood, then a black-and-white hexagon tile wall with a plaster rosette. Steel portals framed in raw timber run down one side, track lighting overhead, a bathroom, and a grey-tile room at the back with a radiator.

The model places each finish along the corridor by eye from the video and the plan. It is a drawing, not a survey.

The white box
1,400sq ft, per Meridian

Peeled back to white

Floors lifted, the tile wall and rosette off, the steel and timber out. White walls, a white ceiling, one pale floor. The track lighting stays: it is the one good idea the room already has.

The walls

Nine prints, one level row

His line drawings in black 16 by 20 frames, hung at one height down the east wall. A white wall behind them, and the yellow tactile strip, the edge of a subway platform, down the floor below. A room you walk down like a gallery.

The rail

Pipe, plank, tees

Matte black pipe and oak plank down the west wall, one colourway to a bay: cream, navy, lilac, moss, peach. Two oak tables carry folded stacks down the middle. Nothing else on the floor.

The screens
15screens, all PJ's animations

Lights down, screens up

A video wall of six at the back that changes what it shows every fourteen seconds: one picture across all six, then six pictures, then prints. Three double-sided screens hang on poles down the corridor. Three more face East Houston from the window. Gumbit, Pizza Rat, Suit Guy, the Murder Hornets loop and the New York loop. The back wall behind them is painted black.

The street

The window is the sign

At night the three screens in the glass do the talking to East Houston. The dots on the street are one weekday, sped up: people scaled to the subway entries at Broadway-Lafayette and Prince Street, cars to the city's traffic count on Houston.
